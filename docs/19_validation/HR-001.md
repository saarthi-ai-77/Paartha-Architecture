# HR-001: Canonical Hypothesis Registry

**Role note:** Produced under the Chief Systems Engineer role for **VP-001 ("Validation Program")**. HR-001 is the central scientific registry tracking every major hypothesis of ACA v1.0, ACA-0, CPA-001, LRS-001, KRS-001, UIT-001, and CUR-001.

Every hypothesis records:
- **Identifier**: Unique tracking ID.
- **Statement**: Precise scientific claim.
- **Supporting Evidence**: Validated empirical studies and mathematical proofs.
- **Contradictory Evidence**: Falsification findings and operating envelope limits.
- **Current Confidence**: Quantified scientific confidence ($0\%-100\%$).
- **Experimental Status**: Validated, Provisional, Falsified, or Open Question.

---

## 1. Primary Hypothesis Registry

### HYP-001: Instruction-Based Knowledge Acquisition
- **Statement**: An artificial cognitive system can acquire executable knowledge, synthesize non-verbatim explanations, transfer concepts to novel entities, evaluate counterfactuals, and request targeted clarifications purely through mentor-guided instruction without statistical next-token pretraining or massive text corpora.
- **Supporting Evidence**: EXP-026 (100.0% pass rate across 5 benchmark stages on ACA-0), LRS-001, KRS-001.
- **Contradictory Evidence**: Tested only in constrained domain (1 lesson benchmark). Scaling to 1,000+ multi-domain lessons untested.
- **Current Confidence**: **85%**
- **Experimental Status**: **Provisional / Promising**

---

### HYP-002: Competence-Gated Memory Allocation (SOS-001 Primary Discipline)
- **Statement**: Writing primary factual entries unconditionally to $S_{episodic}$ on first exposure while $S_{semantic}$ (backbone) updates gradually accommodates single-exposure teaching without write-starvation or gating collapse.
- **Supporting Evidence**: EXP-019, EXP-022 (restored single-exposure recall from 10% to 100% capacity), SOS-001.
- **Contradictory Evidence**: EXP-018 falsified surprise gating under staged non-rehearsed continual learning.
- **Current Confidence**: **95%**
- **Experimental Status**: **Validated**

---

### HYP-003: Structure-Matched Composition (COMPOSE / RC-01)
- **Statement**: Restricting free parameters to a pre-verified operator transform family enables 100% exact-match generalization on structural sequence tasks (e.g. SCAN benchmark).
- **Supporting Evidence**: EXP-002, EXP-020 (100.0% exact match vs Transformer's ~0.71%), EXP-021.
- **Contradictory Evidence**: EXP-023 claims a step-function collapse to 0.0% accuracy at recursion depth $\ge 3$, but EXP-023's `results.json` does not exist in this repository (flagged unverified 2026-07-30, same pattern as EXP-025's confirmed fabrication — see `docs/06_experiments/Completed.md`). Treat the depth-$\le 2$ boundary as a plausible working assumption, not an established one, until re-verified.
- **Current Confidence**: **85%** (the core EXP-002/020/021 result is solid; the specific recursion-depth-$\le 2$ envelope is unverified pending EXP-023's re-run, not the 90% "bounded and confirmed" framing this previously implied).
- **Experimental Status**: **Validated (core claim); Envelope Constraint Unverified**

### HYP-004: Disjoint-Parameter Composability
- **Statement**: Heterogeneous computational pathways with structurally disjoint sub-networks exhibit zero mutual cross-task interference.
- **Supporting Evidence**: EXP-004, EXP-021 (both genuinely validated). The EXP-023 parameter-sharing-ratio citation is unverified (`results.json` absent, flagged 2026-07-30) and should not be counted as independent support until re-run.
- **Contradictory Evidence**: Shared unprojected embeddings undergo gradient competition under a single joint loss function without task projection heads.
- **Current Confidence**: **90%** (unchanged core support from EXP-004/021; EXP-023's additional claim not counted until verified)
- **Experimental Status**: **Validated** (on EXP-004/021's basis, independent of EXP-023)

### HYP-005: Recency-Promotion Safety (CPA-001 Invariant INV-1)
- **Statement**: Continual cognitive architectures require a temporal ordering invariant ($e_{promote}(k) \prec e_{evict}(k)$) ensuring factual entry $k \in S_{episodic}$ completes $S_{semantic}$ promotion before capacity eviction to prevent historical memory loss.
- **Supporting Evidence:** **RETRACTED (corrected 2026-07-30).** Originally cited EXP-024 (1.2% historical recall, unverified — its `results.json` does not exist) and EXP-025 (claimed 1.2% → 100.0%). EXP-025 was directly re-run on its unmodified code: real historical recall is 2.8-10.0% across ALL five policies, with no meaningful separation between INV-1-enforced (5.2%, 3.2%) and INV-1-violated (2.8%, 6.4%, **10.0%** — the highest) conditions. See `docs/06_experiments/Completed.md` (EXP-025, corrected entry).
- **Contradictory Evidence**: The corrected EXP-025 re-run is itself now the primary contradictory evidence — INV-1 enforcement shows no measurable effect on historical recall in the one experiment that directly tested it.
- **Current Confidence**: **10%** (revised from 98%; not zero, since INV-1 remains a plausible, untested-in-its-actual-intended-form hypothesis — the tested implementations in `runtime/sip002/policies.py` may simply not correctly implement what INV-1 requires, which is itself an open question, not a resolved falsification of the underlying idea)
- **Experimental Status**: **Reasoned Hypothesis — NOT Validated.** EXP-024 also remains unverified pending direct re-execution (not yet performed; see `docs/06_experiments/Completed.md`).

### HYP-006: Curriculum Dependency Requirement (CUR-001 Theorem 1.1)
- **Statement**: In a bounded, non-pretrained cognitive architecture, curriculum prerequisite ordering is a formal computational requirement (not an educational convenience) to prevent ungrounded invariants (FL-2a) and premature eviction (PM-1).
- **Supporting Evidence**: CUR-001 formal proof, `runtime/aca0/` topological lesson execution.
- **Contradictory Evidence**: Untested under randomized lesson ordering benchmarks (EXP-035 design).
- **Current Confidence**: **85%**
- **Experimental Status**: **Reasoned Hypothesis**

---

### HYP-007: Adversarial Robustness & Contradiction Detection
- **Statement**: Invariant audits in $S_{invariants}$ detect contradictory or deceptive mentor instructions, triggering `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED` without corrupting existing knowledge.
- **Supporting Evidence**: LRS-001 §8 & §10, EXP-026 Stage 5 (100% clarification synthesis).
- **Contradictory Evidence**: EXP-027 demonstrated that without explicit mutual exclusion / disjointness rules ($\text{Disjoint}(\text{Mammal}, \text{Fish})$) in $S_{invariants}$, category assignment is additive (`['Mammal', 'Fish']`), causing ACA-0 to answer `YES` to both contradictory queries without emitting clarification requests.
- **Current Confidence**: **65%** (adjusted from 75% post EXP-027).
- **Experimental Status**: **Partially Falsified / Uncovered Boundary Limitation**

---

### HYP-008: Long-Term Continual Learning Stability ($1,000+$ Lessons)
- **Statement**: Pre-eviction trigger hooks and persistent staging queues maintain historical competence without catastrophic forgetting across $1,000+$ sequential instructional lessons.
- **Supporting Evidence:** **RETRACTED (corrected 2026-07-30).** Originally cited "EXP-025 300-episode trial (100% historical recall under N=150)" — this misdescribes EXP-025 (which was a 5-policy comparison, not a single 300-episode trial) and cites a number (100%) that does not match even EXP-025's own original claimed figure for its N=150 condition (Policy 3, claimed 98%), let alone the corrected real figure (6.4%). No genuine supporting evidence for this hypothesis currently exists.
- **Contradictory Evidence**: **EXP-031 falsified single-item pre-eviction hooks at scale ($N=1,000 > C=30$)** — genuinely verified (real per-seed data). Achieved only **10.38% overall recall** (6.7% early recall) across 5 seeds due to catastrophic weight overwriting in the backbone LM from unbuffered single-item micro-gradient steps. The corrected EXP-025 (2.8-10.0% across all policies, even at small scale) is now *additional* contradictory evidence, not supporting evidence.
- **Current Confidence**: **5%** (revised further down from 15% — the already-low confidence had partial support from a since-retracted citation; EXP-031 alone, now the only real evidence, is unambiguously negative).
- **Experimental Status**: **FALSIFIED for Isolated Micro-Hooks at scale (EXP-031, verified); also unsupported at small scale (EXP-025, corrected). Requires Historical Replay Buffer — untested, not yet built.**
