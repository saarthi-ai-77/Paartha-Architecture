## 3. Emergent Failure Taxonomy & System-Level Bottleneck

1. **Systemic Recency-Horizon Bottleneck (Architectural Limitation)**:
   When facts in $S_{episodic}$ are evicted under capacity pressure ($N=150 > C=30$) *before* an offline mentor consolidation cycle runs, the mentor module (which scans active $S_{episodic}$ facts) misses the evicted historical facts. As a result, $S_{semantic}$ (the backbone) never receives gradient consolidation for evicted facts, causing historical recall to drop to **1.2%**.
2. **Phase Separation Principle**:
   Online interactive learning in $S_{episodic}$ is recency-bounded by capacity $C$. Offline consolidation into $S_{semantic}$ must be triggered *dynamically prior to eviction* or via an experience buffer, rather than on a static low-frequency schedule.
